Privacy Policy

Text Case Converter Browser Extension

Last Updated: September 11, 2026 | Version: 1.0.0

1. Introduction

Text Case Converter is a lightweight browser extension that converts selected text or pasted input into various casing formats (camelCase, Title Case, snake_case, and more).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is handled, and your rights. We are committed to keeping your data private and secure.

Key Point: The Extension operates entirely on your device with no syncing, telemetry, advertising, analytics, or remote data collection. Nothing you type or convert is ever transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Data

The only data the Extension processes is text you actively enter or select for conversion. This information:

  • is never transmitted off your device;
  • is never read from your system clipboard (the Extension only writes its generated output);
  • is not used for profiling or automated decision-making.

2.2 Technical Data

The Extension requests the following browser permissions, used strictly for functionality without external data transmission:

Manifest Permissions

PermissionPurpose
storage Persists your input, output, case preference, and theme locally.
contextMenus Creates the right-click "Convert selected text" submenu.
scripting Injects a clipboard helper into the active tab for context menu conversion.

Host Permissions

<all_urls> allows the clipboard helper to function on any tab. The Extension never reads, modifies, or exfiltrates page content.

2.3 Usage Data

We do not collect usage data such as which casing options you choose, usage frequency, session duration, or analytics. No telemetry or crash reporting is sent.

3. Data Storage & Transmission

Data is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • remains on your device and is not synced to your Google/Microsoft account;
  • is not encrypted by the Extension (protected by your operating system);
  • is never sent over the network (the Extension contains no networking code).

Clear your data anytime using the in-app Clear button, removing the Extension, or via your browser's extension-data controls.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide Extension functionality:

  • Convert and display transformed text in real time.
  • Restore your last input, output, and theme between sessions.
  • Enable right-click conversion via the clipboard helper.

We do not use your data for marketing, personalization, advertising, research, or commercial purposes.

5. Legal Basis & User Rights

Because processing is strictly local and no personal data leaves your device, the following rights apply:

GDPR (European Economic Area)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the lawful basis for processing your entered text is the performance of the service you requested. You have the right to:

  • Access your data (visible in input/output fields);
  • Rectification (edit and re-save your text);
  • Erasure (click Clear, remove the Extension, or clear browser storage);
  • Data portability (copy text from the output field);
  • Restriction/objection (uninstall the Extension anytime).

CCPA/CPRA (California, USA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to deletion, and the right to opt out of personal information sales. The Extension does not sell personal information or disclose it for commercial purposes. Use the Clear button or remove the Extension to exercise deletion rights.

6. Data Retention

Personal text is retained locally only as long as you keep it. Clearing fields, removing the Extension, or clearing browser storage deletes it. There is no automated retention schedule.

7. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not integrate with third-party services, content-delivery networks, analytics providers, advertising networks, or social plugins. No third party can access your data.

8. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted information through the Extension.
